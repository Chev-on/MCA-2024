# Music Curation and Analytics Portfolio - Vivaldi

## Introduction / Week 1
### Theme - Vivaldi and his Four Seasons
The theme I have chosen for my data set is focused on the collection of pieces commonly known as "The Four Seasons" by the composer Antonio Vivaldi. Antonio Vivaldi was a very successful Italian composer and violin virtuoso in the Baroque era of music (17th - early 18th century). In his time, Vivaldi was a very busy composer and performer and is partially credited for the development of classical music, introducing and changing orchestral playing and violin techniques. In his lifetime Vivaldi composed over 500 concerti most of which were for string orchestra and soloist and solo violin. One of the most famous being a set of four violin concertos known as _Le quattro stagioni_, The Four Seasons[^1].

- **Access: Limits of Copyright Law**

  Copyright laws tells us that artistic works are free to the public domain 70 years after the authours lifetime[^2]. This means all of Vivaldi's published works are available for public use, and are easily accessable. However, this does not apply to recordings or arrangements of his pieces.
   Considering Vivaldi's life ended much before audio recording was even invented (1857/1877)[^3], much of the recordings available today are still under copyright law. This can greatly limit public use and access.

- **Curation: Music from the 17th Century**

  Through the years preservation of the arts has been a consistent challenge for researchers, historians and artists. Whether through years of war or lack of technology much art has been lost to history. Vivaldi wrote many pieces of music throughout his life and it is unknown if there are some which may have been incorrectly archived or misinterpreted.
  The challange with this is that there is not a sure way of knowing if an interpreted performance is exactly the way Vivaldi intented. One *could* skim through pages and pages of Italian notes and directions but this would be very difficult to do for a 40 minute piece like The Four Seasons.

- **Distribution: Music Organisations**

  Most of the sheet music and audio files I used were sourced from IMSLP, the International Music Score Library Project[^4].
